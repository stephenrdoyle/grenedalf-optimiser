Depth bounds per pool

grenedalf has a single depth filter for all pools, so each pool's own range is applied as a mask: a site outside any pool's range is masked in every pool, so all pools are called at the same sites. grenedalf's own --filter-sample-min-read-depth / --filter-sample-max-read-depth are set to 14 / 266, wide enough not to undo these. n_eff is the number of chromosomes effectively sampled at that depth from a pool of that size; depth well beyond the pool size adds little. Each pool's own bounds remove 4–6% of the accessible genome; the shared mask removes 23% (3.7× the largest single pool), because the pools are out of range in different places. Most of that is usually low depth; see Where the mask falls for whether it is long blocks in single pools (divergent haplotypes, deletions) or scattered.

Coverage variance

What kind of depth variation there is decides what a depth bound removes: sampling noise (harmless, and the estimators handle it), regional blocks shared by all pools (reference or technical: mask), or blocks in single pools (biology: report).

  • Spread: autosomal coverage varies 3.4× more than read sampling alone would (CV 0.35 against 0.11 for pure sampling noise). Fixed-fold bounds assume sampling-like spread; with this much more, 0.5× the median sits closer to the middle of the distribution than 2× does, so far more is cut below than above.
  • Scale: 50% of the per-base variance persists in 1 kb means and 17% in 10 kb means. Depth varies mostly in regional blocks (mapping, divergence, GC, copy number), not as scattered noise.
  • Low depth: 50% of the 1 kb bins below a pool's lower bound lie in runs of at least 5 kb. Of a pool's low bins, 100% are low in that pool only (population divergence, deletions: biology to report, not to mask everywhere) and 0% are low in at least half the pools (reference or technical).
  • Between pools: 1 kb depth correlates at 0.88 (median), so most coverage structure is shared by all pools.

X chromosome and mitochondrion

X chromosome

Males carry one X, so in a pool with males the X has lower depth and is sampled from fewer copies. With a fraction m of males, X depth / autosome depth = 1 − m/2 (0.75 for a 50:50 pool), and the X pool size is the pool size × (1 − m/2). The ratio is taken between the peaks of the two depth distributions, over well-mapped bins, because medians are pulled down wherever reads fail to map. MALE_FRACTION, when set, is used instead, with the estimate kept as a check.
